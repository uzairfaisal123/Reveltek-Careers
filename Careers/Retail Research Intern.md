# Retail Research Intern - Reveltek

**Job Title:** Retail Research Intern
**Company:** Reveltek
**Location:** Remote
**Division:** Home Goods & Electronics

---



## Position Overview

Reveltek is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Retail Research Intern to join our team remotely. This internship offers valuable, hands-on learning experience within our Home Goods & Electronics division. The successful candidate will play a crucial role in researching a diverse range of products – from consumer electronics like laptops, tablets, and phones to home essentials such as paintings, kitchenware, decorations, and smart home devices. This research is vital for facilitating the setup and ongoing needs of our Homes & Business Operations across our US locations.

## Responsibilities

* Conduct thorough market research on specified home goods and electronics products.
* Utilize various online retail platforms, including Alibaba, Amazon, Lowes, Home Depot, TJ Maxx, JC Penney, Kohls, and others, to gather product information.
* Analyze and compare products based on factors like price, quality, features, availability, and vendor reliability.
* Identify potential suppliers and gather quotes or pricing information.
* Compile research findings into organized reports, spreadsheets, or comparison matrices.
* Assist the Home Goods & Electronics team in identifying optimal products to meet the operational needs of our US locations.
* Stay informed about current trends in consumer electronics and home goods markets.
* Perform other related duties as assigned.

## Qualifications

* Currently enrolled in or a recent graduate of an accredited degree program (Fields such as Business, Marketing, Supply Chain Management, Retail Management, or related areas are a plus, but not required).
* Strong analytical and research skills with a keen eye for detail.
* Excellent organizational and time management skills.
* Proficient in navigating and utilizing online search engines and e-commerce websites.
* Familiarity with online retailers like Alibaba, Amazon, Lowes, Home Depot, etc., is highly desirable.
* Solid written and verbal communication skills.
* Ability to work independently and manage tasks effectively in a remote setting.
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (especially Excel) or Google Workspace (especially Sheets).
* A proactive attitude and eagerness to learn.

## What We Offer

* Direct hands-on experience in retail product research within a business operations context.
* Exposure to sourcing strategies for a wide variety of goods.
* Opportunity to make tangible contributions to the company's operational success.
* Mentorship opportunities within the Home Goods & Electronics division.
* A flexible remote work environment.
